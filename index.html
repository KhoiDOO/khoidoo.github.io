About Me

I am a CS PhD Student at Trinity College Dublin, working in the 3D Graphics and Vision under the supervision of Dr. Binh-Son Hua. My research centers on 3D Generation and 3D Reconstruction. Specifically, I investigate 3D Reconstruction, Gaussian Splatting, Isosurfacing, and Autoregressive Models for high-fidelity 3D geometric synthesis.
